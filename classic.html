Beginner mode accepts partially formed signs and smaller movements. Practice one sign to focus recognition while you learn. Switch to Balanced if it speaks when you did not intend a sign.

Try this

  1. Raise the hand you want to use. Keep your movements comfortable.
  2. For YES, loosely close your hand and make a small wrist nod.
  3. For HELLO, open your hand and wave gently side to side.
  4. For THANK YOU, bring a relaxed open hand near your mouth or chin, then sweep it away and a little down. No starting hold needed; keep your face visible.
  5. Pause briefly between messages; you do not have to open your hand.

For NO, close your index and middle fingers toward your thumb, with the other fingers curled. Watch YES. Watch NO. Watch HELLO. Watch THANK YOU.
