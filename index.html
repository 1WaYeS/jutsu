The recognizer allows modest variation in finger position and movement. It still needs a recognizable shape and motion. These are isolated gesture approximations; a wave for HELLO is a familiar greeting, not a substitute for learning ASL. Reference links open ASL University’s dictionary.

Learn-only entries are not detected by the main camera. Sentence-level recognition is not enabled here. Personal examples remain available separately in Train AI.
